# Product

## Register

product

## Users

- Buyers who want to find products, compare listings, and contact sellers quickly.
- People looking for local services like doctors, plumbers, lawyers, mechanics, and technicians.
- Merchants and professionals who need a public business profile, product catalog, and service storefront in one account.
- Platform admins who need verification, moderation, dispute handling, and trust controls.

## Product Purpose

Richkem Services is a Kenyan marketplace platform that combines ecommerce, service discovery, and business storefronts in one place.

It should let people:

- buy ordinary products and high-value assets
- discover and contact service providers
- create a storefront like Shopify
- publish a public profile like Google Business Profile
- pay and transact with trust, especially for expensive or service-based work

Success means the site feels faster, clearer, more premium, and more trustworthy than common marketplace competitors.

## Brand Personality

- Trustworthy
- Premium
- Practical

The experience should feel confident, modern, and direct. It should help users act quickly without looking noisy, cheap, or generic.

## Anti-references

- Plain HTML-looking marketplace pages
- Generic Jumia/Jiji clones
- Over-decorated dashboards and random visual effects
- Template-style ecommerce pages with no trust signals
- Confusing navigation between products and services
- Unclear contact or checkout flows

## Design Principles

1. Make trust visible early through verification, contact methods, and clear business identity.
2. Treat products and services as different buying modes, not the same listing type.
3. Keep the experience mobile-first and fast to scan.
4. Make seller tools feel like a real storefront system, not just a form.
5. Use strong hierarchy so users always know what to do next.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Target WCAG AA contrast.
- Keep touch targets large enough for mobile use.
- Support reduced motion.
- Make all important actions available by text, not only icons.
- Keep language simple and easy to scan.
